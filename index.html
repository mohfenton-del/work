Professional Bio & Profile

ENGLISH • EN

I am Mohammed Hatem Al-Shahrani, a dedicated Sound Engineer, Sound Operator, and Sound Mixer pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Media (Audio-Visual Production) at King Abdulaziz University (Student ID: 2441068 | Section: 11-12).

I have contributed to numerous narrative and short films, documentaries, commercials, and podcasts across diverse genres. My hands-on experience spans multiple sectors of audio production—including on-set field recording and boom operation, dialogue cleanup and spectral restoration, custom sound effects (SFX) design, musical scoring, and final mastering—combining creative problem-solving with solid production fundamentals.
